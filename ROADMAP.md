# Roadmap

## 0.2 — Combat depth
- Primary Target (Soldier): free Strike prompt on area attacks, success→failure downgrade on hit
- Operative/Envoy/Mystic/Witchwarper/Technomancer class-resource HUDs (verify each feature's data in sf2e first)
- Cover/range helper overlay for ranged-heavy fights
- Optional Sequencer/JB2A hooks for Area Fire, Auto-Fire, solar weapon

## 0.3 — Starship scenes
- Scene presets saved as Journal pages (import/export stat blocks)
- Threat routine runner: per-round routine reminders and quick-roll buttons
- Player-facing role picker (each PC picks role at turn start from their own client)
- Starship HUD on the combat tracker

## 0.4 — Living galaxy (immersion)
- Drift travel tracker tied to the world clock (flavor events table, arrival announcements)
- Station/planet ambience presets (playlist + lighting + scene notes)
- NPC memory system (port from PFK) and comms contact book
- Ship VI personality profiles for `/ai`

## 0.5 — Downtime & economy
- Gear upgrade/potency crystal planner with credit cost previews
- Starfinder Society mission log (fame/boons notes alongside system chronicles)
- Faction-linked contacts and rewards

## Player-facing
- Printable player cheat sheet (actions, conditions, area attacks, starship roles)
- GM prep packet template per session
